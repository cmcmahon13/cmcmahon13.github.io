Hi, I’m Casey McMahon.

Behavioral science · Organizational research · Quantitative methods

I am a Behavioral Scientist who studies how people respond to workplace policies, organizational change, and social environments.

I design studies, analyze behavioral and survey data, and build reproducible research workflows that explain what shapes trust, attitudes, and decision-making at work using methods including survey experiments, longitudinal designs, statistical modeling, text analysis, and qualitative research.
